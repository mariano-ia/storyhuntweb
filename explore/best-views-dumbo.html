Best Views in DUMBO

DUMBO, short for Down Under the Manhattan Bridge Overpass, is a gem of Brooklyn that often gets overshadowed by its more popular counterparts. While tourists flock to the iconic views from Pebble Beach and Brooklyn Bridge Park, many miss the lesser-known vantage points that offer a unique perspective of the New York skyline and the East River. The area is rich in history, having transformed from an industrial hub into a vibrant neighborhood filled with art and culture. One spot that often goes unnoticed is the corner of Washington Street and Water Street, where you can capture the Manhattan Bridge framed perfectly with the old brick buildings. Additionally, the rooftop of the Time Out Market at 55 Water Street provides a stunning panoramic view of the city, especially during sunset when the skyline glows in hues of orange and pink, making it an ideal spot for photographers and romantics alike.

To truly experience the best views in DUMBO, consider visiting these specific locations: 1) Brooklyn Bridge Park (Main Street Park at Pier 1) offers a breathtaking view of the Manhattan skyline, particularly at dusk. 2) Jane’s Carousel, located at 98 Pier 1, not only features a vintage carousel but also a stunning backdrop of the Brooklyn and Manhattan Bridges. 3) The staircase at Washington Street and Main Street is one of the most photographed spots, providing a classic NYC postcard view. 4) Visit the rooftop bar at the 1 Hotel Brooklyn Bridge (60 Furman Street) for a luxurious experience with cocktails and unparalleled views of the waterfront. 5) For a hidden gem, check out the view from 25 Washington Street, where the old factories provide a unique contrast to the modern skyline. The best times to visit these spots are early morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds and catch the best light.
