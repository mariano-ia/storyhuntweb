Best Speakeasies in Greenwich Village

Greenwich Village, a historic bastion of bohemian culture, was a central hub for speakeasies during the Prohibition era. While many visitors flock to the more commercial venues, they often overlook the hidden gems that still evoke the clandestine charm of the 1920s. Along streets like MacDougal and Bleecker, you can find unmarked doors and vintage decor that transport you back in time. For instance, at 75.5 MacDougal Street lies the iconic 'Employees Only', a speakeasy that embodies the spirit of the era with its dimly lit ambiance and expertly crafted cocktails. Another must-visit is 'The Garret', perched above a Five Guys at 206 Avenue A, where the entrance is cleverly disguised yet the drinks are anything but ordinary. These spots not only serve drinks but also tell the story of Greenwich Village's rich history, making them essential stops for anyone wanting to grasp the essence of New York's hidden nightlife.

1. **Employees Only** (75.5 MacDougal Street) – Known for its classic cocktails and vibrant atmosphere, this speakeasy is a staple in the neighborhood. Visit during the week to avoid long waits. 2. **The Garret** (206 Avenue A) – This hidden bar, above a fast-food joint, serves creative cocktails in a cozy setting. Go early in the evening for a more relaxed vibe. 3. **Blind Barber** (339 E 10th Street) – A barbershop by day and a speakeasy by night, look for the secret door leading to a lively cocktail lounge. Best to visit on weekends for the full experience. 4. **PDT (Please Don’t Tell)** (113 St. Marks Place) – Enter through a phone booth inside Crif Dogs to access this renowned speakeasy. Reservations recommended, especially on Fridays and Saturdays. 5. **The Flatiron Room** (37 W 26th Street) – A bit further afield, this spot brings live jazz into the mix with a fantastic whiskey selection. Ideal for a late-night unwind after exploring the Village. Each location offers a unique slice of New York's vibrant history, making them perfect for an authentic speakeasy experience.
